Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

1. Data Controller

Lusitania Medieval is the controller responsible for processing personal data collected through this website for the purposes described in this policy.

2. Data Collected

We may collect the following data when a user completes the contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Type of event
  • Number of participants
  • Message submitted

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

Data is processed to respond to requests for information, provide proposals and manage pre-contractual communications. The legal bases are steps taken prior to entering into a contract and our legitimate interest in managing commercial enquiries.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or to comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing

The contact form uses Formspree to process submissions. Data may be hosted and processed by this provider in accordance with its privacy policy and applicable legal safeguards.

6. Data Subject Rights

As provided by law, data subjects may request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of their data, or object to its processing, through Lusitania Medieval's official contact channels.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are implemented to protect personal data against loss, unauthorised access, alteration or improper disclosure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. The version in effect is the version published on this page.

9. Cookies

This website uses analytics cookies (Google Analytics) only with the user's prior consent. These cookies are disabled by default.

Accepting via the cookie banner enables statistical measurement to improve the website. If consent is declined, browsing remains available without analytics tracking.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookies" button displayed on the site.
