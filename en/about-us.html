Our Story

About Us

Lusitania Medieval specialises in historical reenactment and immersive themed events, born from a passion for reviving the mysticism, nobility and cultural heritage of the Middle Ages.

Our Roots

We entered the medieval world in 2018, initially focused on medieval combat and tournament organisation. This foundation gave us discipline, technical knowledge and direct practical experience of historical reenactment.

At the end of 2025, we created Lusitania Medieval and expanded our scope to develop richer, more immersive experiences tailored to different audiences and occasions.

What Sets Us Apart

We combine rigorous respect for history with powerful entertainment. Our aim is not merely to represent the Middle Ages, but to transform them into a memorable, safe and deeply engaging experience.

We create interactive encampments, breathtaking knight combat and tailored entertainment that respects historical context without losing its impact for modern audiences.

Our Mission

Our mission is to transport audiences through time with experiences that bring together heritage, spectacle and participation. We want every person to feel they have entered a place where history gains form, voice and presence.

We create distinctive events that engage companies, families, couples, municipalities and communities through narratives that leave a lasting impression.

More Than an Event

More than planning events, Lusitania Medieval builds entire kingdoms where history is not simply told, but lived, felt and celebrated.

This vision guides every production, performance and scenic detail we bring into the field.
