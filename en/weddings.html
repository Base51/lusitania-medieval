Medieval Weddings: A Love for Eternity

Celebrate your union with the solemnity, magic and immersive spirit of the Middle Ages.

Your Historical Fairytale

If you have always dreamed of a wedding far from the conventional, Lusitania Medieval transports your “I do” to an age of courtly romance, legends and ancestral rituals.

We create a mystical and memorable setting where every detail respects the aesthetics and spirit of the Middle Ages.

What the Authentic Royal Experience Includes

  • Ancestral rituals: a recreation of traditional handfasting or blessings inspired by medieval customs.
  • Entertainment and spectacle: medieval combat duels between armoured knights, interactive medieval games for guests and a medieval encampment.
  • Triumphal entrance and honour guard: guests are welcomed by armoured knights bearing standards, with an honour guard for the couple at key moments.

We can also help with attire choices for the couple and guests, and arrange partnerships with live medieval musicians.

An Unforgettable Day

Your love deserves more than an ordinary celebration. It deserves to enter history.

We bring your vision to life so that you and your guests feel transported to the Middle Ages.
